How It Works

From Cosmetic Grading to Predictive Battery Governance

Phase 01 · Year 1

UK Soft Launch & Product Validation

A London-focused launch trains the first machine-learning models on UK-specific secondary market data smartphone refurbishment behaviours, distributor batch testing and incoming UK/EU compliance requirements. High-intensity pilots run across 5–10 London-based refurbishment facilities.

Founder Faisal Rashid Sunka leads founder-led sales and pilot deployment, completing deep-dive market research interviews with 150+ potential UK SME refurbishers and confirming replacement authenticity forensics models on real UK secondary electronics datasets.

Phase 02 · Year 2

Full UK Rollout & Model Refinement

The platform scales to 50–100 professional refurbishers and secondary distributors across Manchester, Birmingham and Bristol, with plug-and-play connectivity to major UK marketplaces, POS systems and inventory management tools.

The team grows to 4–6, adding a senior data scientist and full-stack engineer to refine the Replacement Authenticity Assessment Framework and post-sale outcome feedback models, while recurring SaaS subscriptions scale across all pricing tiers.

Phase 03 · Year 3

Scale, National Leadership & EU Expansion

Battery Passport Network grows to 200+ UK professional firms, finalises the Federated Risk Benchmarking Network, and launches an API licensing model for third-party device insurers, warranty providers and marketplaces.

The business becomes a recognised UK battery-risk certification platform, with market research and pilot programmes underway for expansion into EU circular economy hubs such as Berlin, Paris and Stockholm.
