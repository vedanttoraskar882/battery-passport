The Full Platform

The AI-Driven Battery Risk Intelligence Engine

Battery Passport Network bridges the "trust gap" in the UK circular economy through an integrated, closed-loop certification platform combining electrochemical diagnostics, replacement provenance and commercial resale outcomes into a single cloud-native, mobile-first dashboard for refurbishers, distributors and ITAD agencies.

Who It's For

Built for Every Player in the Battery Value Chain.

SME Smartphone Refurbishers

Independent operators needing a standardised, predictive risk taxonomy (BPN-A to BPN-X) to accurately price inventory and protect margins from unexpected returns.

ITAD & Enterprise Buyback Programmes

Mid-market organisations handling high-volume corporate trade-ins, needing unified API-driven commercial memory across multiple processing sites.

Secondary Electronics Distributors

High-volume logistics hubs where failure forensics and replacement authenticity assessments are critical to avoiding hidden, unsafe battery replacements.

Device Insurers & Warranty Providers

Financial and operational businesses needing to accurately price warranty premiums based on true predictive residual life, not external cosmetic condition.

Recyclers & Second-Life Operators

Regulators & Policy Makers
