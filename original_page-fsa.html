Battery Regulation Compliance

Battery Passport Network's compliance module is designed to help UK secondary electronics operators stay aligned with the incoming 2026/2027 EU/UK Battery Regulations, WEEE e-waste directives and ICO data-wiping guidance though the operator remains legally responsible for on-site compliance.

Digitised Battery-Risk Records

Every diagnostic, replacement and resale decision is logged with timestamps, replacing paper technician notes with an auditable digital trail.

Hidden Risk Alerts

Operators are alerted in real time when a battery shows signs of unverified replacement, thermal instability or counterfeit components.

Safety & Traceability

The Replacement Authenticity Assessment Framework and BPN-A to BPN-X taxonomy provide a robust, audit-ready trail for regulatory enquiries.

Audit-Ready Records

Compliance history is exportable for environmental and consumer protection audits, with a full timestamped record per device batch.
