Our Core Capabilities

One Platform. Every Battery Decision.

Battery Passport Network links electrochemical diagnostics, replacement provenance and post-sale outcomes into one closed-loop commercial platform diagnosing battery risk before it erodes margin, not after.

Digital Identity
Module 01

Battery Passport Network

Fingerprints the behavioural and evidential profile of every battery, benchmarking degradation, replacement history and safety signals into a machine-readable trust identity.

Each processed device receives a standardised BPN-A to BPN-X classification, encoding electrochemical signals, technician repair records and post-sale performance into one commercial identity that travels with the device.
Authenticity
Module 02

Replacement Authenticity Assessment

Detects the root causes of hidden battery risk by analysing the delta between claimed replacement history and actual thermal/electrochemical behaviour.

Identifies verified, suspicious or unsafe components protecting refurbishers from unverified counterfeit replacements that drive the 70% post-sale return bottleneck.
Prediction
Module 03

Residual Commercial Life Prediction

Simulates resale configurations using historical performance and post-sale outcome data to forecast the estimated months of commercial acceptability.

Connects initial battery conditions directly to financial outcomes giving small refurbishers the strategic risk intelligence usually reserved for multinational buyback programmes.
Resale Engine
Module 04

Battery Resale Eligibility Engine

Automatically decomposes complex batches of used devices into a structured resale decision from Premium BPN-A to Blocked BPN-X with no subjective technician interpretation.

Lets UK refurbishers scale second-life device volume without the crippling 70% post-sale return and warranty dispute bottleneck that currently stifles profitability.
Benchmarking
Module 05

Federated Risk Benchmarking Network

Shares anonymised Battery Passport Networks from thousands of UK refurbishers and distributors, continually improving predictive models without exposing sensitive business data.

The platform's defensible moat every processed device makes the model smarter, letting an independent operator in Manchester benefit from failure trends observed in London.
Compliance
Module 06

Operational Compliance Reporting

Delivers audit-ready reports proving battery safety and traceability, aligned with incoming 2026/2027 EU/UK Battery Regulations and ICO data-wiping standards.

Maintains a high-fidelity digital log of every battery-risk interaction no more paper trails lost at the bottom of a drawer when a compliance audit arrives.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does Battery Passport Network automate?

+
Battery Passport Network automates AI-driven battery-risk classification, replacement authenticity forensics, residual commercial life prediction and compliance reporting unifying diagnostic data normally spread across 4–6 disconnected tools into one dashboard for QC technicians and operations managers.

Do I need to replace my existing diagnostic tools?

+
No. Battery Passport Network is device and diagnostic agnostic, ingesting data via CSV uploads or API connections from your existing testing software so you keep your current stack while gaining commercial trust identity intelligence on top of it.

How is this different from a static battery health percentage?

+
Most diagnostic tools report a single static state-of-health percentage. Battery Passport Network analyses behavioural, thermal and evidential attributes to distinguish a stable original battery from a fast-degrading unverified replacement even when both show the same generic health reading.

What results can I realistically expect?

+
The platform is designed to reduce post-sale battery returns and warranty claims by 20–35%, and diagnostic bottlenecks by 15–25%, with a typical UK SME refurbisher reaching positive ROI within 2–3 months of onboarding.

Is Battery Passport Network compliant with UK/EU regulations?

+
Yes. The platform is built around the incoming 2026/2027 EU/UK Battery Regulations, UK e-waste directives and Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) guidance on data wiping, with automated, audit-ready compliance records.

How long does onboarding take?

+
Once your first diagnostic testing software and inventory management integration is stabilised, the configuration template of the Battery Passport Network is standardised reducing onboarding for subsequent sites from weeks to hours.
