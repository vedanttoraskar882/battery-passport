Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. Battery Passport Network ("BPN", "we", "us") is committed to protecting the personal and operational data of our customers, site users and website visitors in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data We Collect

Account and billing details, battery diagnostic and inventory data entered by operators, device replacement and repair records connected via integrations, and standard website analytics such as device type and pages visited.

2. How We Use Data

To operate the AI battery-risk classification and forensics engine, generate BPN-A to BPN-X reports for your inventory, provide customer support, and improve our models using aggregated, de-identified operational patterns via the Federated Risk Benchmarking Network.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal or business data. Data is shared only with sub-processors required to run the platform (cloud hosting, diagnostic integration partners) under signed data processing agreements.

4. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and can object to certain processing. Contact us at batterypassportnetworkuk@outlook.com to exercise these rights.

5. Data Retention & Security

Operational data is retained for the duration of your subscription plus a limited period for legal and accounting purposes, and is encrypted in transit and at rest.
