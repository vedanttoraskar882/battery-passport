About Battery Passport Network

Turning Hidden Battery Risk Into Verifiable Commercial Trust.

Battery Passport Network builds the world's most trusted digital ecosystem to create, verify and share battery lifecycle data across the value chain. Our AI-driven, end-to-end battery-risk intelligence platform captures, benchmarks and standardises the hidden electrochemical degradation of secondary batteries before a device is resold turning a fragmented, uncertain diagnostic telemetry stream into permanent, verifiable organisational "Commercial Trust Equity."

Vision & Mission
Mission

Enabling UK SMEs to Govern Their Own Battery Risk Intelligence

To enable UK SME refurbishers and secondary distributors to capture, standardise and govern their battery risk intelligence, maximising resale margins, eliminating unpredictable post-sale returns, and establishing verifiable Commercial Trust Equity.

Vision

The Global Standard for Secondary Battery Certification

We aspire to be the global standard for secondary battery-risk certification, fostering a future in which the circular electronics economy operates with absolute battery provenance, consistent post-sale reliability and sustainable, data-driven e-waste reduction.
