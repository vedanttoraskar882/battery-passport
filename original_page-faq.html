Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does Battery Passport Network automate?

+
Battery Passport Network automates AI-driven battery-risk classification, replacement authenticity forensics, residual commercial life prediction and compliance reporting unifying diagnostic data normally spread across 4–6 disconnected tools into one dashboard for QC technicians and operations managers.

Do I need to replace my existing diagnostic tools?

+
No. Battery Passport Network is device and diagnostic agnostic, ingesting data via CSV uploads or API connections from your existing testing software so you keep your current stack while gaining commercial trust identity intelligence on top of it.

How is this different from a static battery health percentage?

+
Most diagnostic tools report a single static state-of-health percentage. Battery Passport Network analyses behavioural, thermal and evidential attributes to distinguish a stable original battery from a fast-degrading unverified replacement even when both show the same generic health reading.

What results can I realistically expect?

+
The platform is designed to reduce post-sale battery returns and warranty claims by 20–35%, and diagnostic bottlenecks by 15–25%, with a typical UK SME refurbisher reaching positive ROI within 2–3 months of onboarding.

Is Battery Passport Network compliant with UK/EU regulations?

+
Yes. The platform is built around the incoming 2026/2027 EU/UK Battery Regulations, UK e-waste directives and Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) guidance on data wiping, with automated, audit-ready compliance records.

How long does onboarding take?

+
Once your first diagnostic testing software and inventory management integration is stabilised, the configuration template of the Battery Passport Network is standardised reducing onboarding for subsequent sites from weeks to hours.
